# Future Game Ideas

A running list of minigame concepts and ideas to revisit.

## Title TBD

You’re in a treehouse overlooking a yard full of characters. Throw water balloons from the treehouse until every character is soaked out.

Track how long it takes to soak everyone.

## Sneaky Car

Drive an RC car through the neighborhood, sneak into a house, and reach the pantry to collect a tasty treat. Watch for characters walking around and stay out of sight so they don’t spot the car.

## Pond Fishing

Cast your line into a pond and watch for the bite indicator. When it appears, reel in before the response window closes to see what you caught.

Catches include little fish, big fish, old boots, and random trash.

## River Crossing

In this Frogger-style game, guide your character from one riverbank to the other. Time your steps onto drifting branches and use stationary rocks as safe spots along the way. Reach the far bank without falling into the water.

## Target Blaster

Shoot bursts of water from a Super Soaker while standing in a fixed spot. Targets include cans, bottles, and red-and-white bullseyes that slide back and forth. Each wave ends when its timer runs out.

A meter shows the gun's pressure. Every shot fires the same burst and uses pressure. Start with a tentative cost of 10% of the full meter per shot. Pump between shots to build pressure. When there's too little pressure for another shot, pump before firing again. Pressure resets between waves.

## Snowball Fight

Characters throw snowballs at each other, dodging incoming throws as they try to land hits.

## Prop Hunt

Hide by disguising yourself as an everyday object. Seekers search the map for disguised players before time runs out.

## Racetrack Builder

Build a single-lane, closed-loop track for a Hot Wheels-style toy car. Pick a starting spot, then add one piece at a time from the open end, like classic RollerCoaster Tycoon. Pieces include straights, curves, uphill curves, climbs, descents, vertical loops, and boosters that speed up the car. Reconnect the last piece to the start to close the circuit.

Test the track in a physics simulation. Show the whole course, give the car a starting push, and watch it run on its own. The car can stall on a climb, fall out of a loop if it’s too slow, or fly off a curve if it’s too fast. The simulation stays open until the player stops it, even if the car stalls or derails. Return to the builder to adjust the layout and test again. Save tracks for later editing and testing.

## Pool Noodle Chop

Set up a timing contest in the backyard. Each player stands at a chopping station with a toy foam sword. A rack above each station drops a pool noodle with a stripe around it. Swing as the stripe passes through the blade's path.

The closer the cut is to the stripe, the more points it earns, up to 20 for a perfect cut. Cutting above the stripe costs points, and missing the noodle costs even more. Highest total after five rounds wins.

## Backyard Apple Catch

Move left and right under a backyard apple tree with a basket. Catch the falling apples and dodge the pinecones mixed in with them. Each apple adds one to your total, and catching a pinecone costs five apples.

One hundred apples fall over the round. The player with the highest total at the end wins.

## Backyard Flier

Fly a helicopter through an endlessly scrolling backyard. Tap to rise and time your taps to slip through gaps between tree branches and shrubs. See how many gaps you can clear before crashing.

## Pipe Crane

Trenches are already dug on a building site. Pick up pipe pieces (straights, T's, corners) with a crane, turn them and lower them into the trenches. After a countdown the water starts flowing; join pipe A to pipe B before it spills out of an open end.

## Perch Hop

A side-on platform race in the backyard. Run, jump and double-jump around the yard and land on each of the ten perches (picnic table, birdhouse, swing, lily pads, branches…) as fast as you can. Each difficulty has its own stage; fall in the pond and it's a splash.

## Acorn Drop

A pegboard game on a painted garden fence. Walk your critter out along the top rail with an acorn and drop it; it bounces down the pegs into a flower pot worth one to five flowers, and the critter heads back to its ledge for the next one. Five acorns, and every pot grows something.

## Perfect Pizza

A little pizza kitchen for young kids. A customer holds up a picture (a pizza with three pepperonis and two olives) and you make it: carry the dough to the pan, then a spoonful of marinara, then a handful of cheese, then count on the toppings in the picture. Into the oven, wait for the ring, and take it out. No time limit, but leave it in too long and it burns: into the bin and make another.

## Leaf Piles

Four players share an autumn backyard, seen from above, one corner each. Leaves keep dropping from the trees all around. Rake them into your corner, and out of everyone else's. Golden leaves are worth five, and a leaf blower turns up now and then. Whatever is in your corner at the whistle counts; best of three rounds.
