# Character asset

`soldier.glb` is the textured, rigged Vanguard soldier with Idle, Walk and Run clips from the official Three.js examples:
https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/dev/examples/models/gltf/Soldier.glb

Downloaded November 1, 2026. The source contains the Mixamo skeleton and Vanguard materials. Mixamo permits characters and animations in games under its royalty-free terms:
https://helpx.adobe.com/creative-cloud/faq/mixamo-faq.html

The accompanying Three.js repository license is included in THREE-LICENSE.txt. That license does not replace Adobe's character/animation terms. This asset is integrated into MINI GAMES, not offered as a standalone asset product.

MINI GAMES adds separate team materials, equipment, independent skeleton instances, animation blending, and procedural crouch/crawl/sidearm posing.
