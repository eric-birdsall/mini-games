# How to Play

Every Mini Game in one place: the goal and the basic controls. Each game also has its own **View How to Play** on its pause screen.

Games marked **(WIP)** are still being built and sit behind the menu's Work in Progress button. Games marked **(keyboard only)** need a keyboard and mouse, so they're greyed out on phones and tablets.

## Controller basics

Every game works with an Xbox controller. Unless a game says otherwise:

| Controller | Does | Keyboard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Left stick / D-pad | Move, steer, aim | WASD or arrow keys |
| Right stick | Look around | I J K L |
| A / RT | Main action (jump, drop, throw, fire) | Space |
| X / LT | Second action (pump, turn, switch) | R |
| B / Menu | Pause | Esc |

On menus and results screens, the stick moves between buttons and A presses them.

## Arena games

First-person and third-person games played in the 3D backyard. Choose Practice, Solo against a bot, or a private match with friends.

### Backyard Hide & Seek (main game)
- **Goal:** Sneakers (blue) sneak cookies from the cookie jars; Seekers (coral) guard the jars, set gadgets and catch the Sneaker. Win by grabbing the cookies, catching the other side, or holding out until time runs out.
- **Controls:** WASD walk, mouse look, Shift sprint, Space jump, C crouch (hold to lie down). 1–5 pick a gadget, Tab / R cycle gadgets, left-click or F uses it. Hold E at a cookie jar to raise the flag. C / V enter and leave camera feeds. Esc releases the mouse.

### Splash (keyboard only, WIP)
- **Goal:** The kid-friendly version of Hide & Seek with water balloons. Three splashes and you're soaked and out.
- **Controls:** As above. Click (or RT) throws a balloon; hold right mouse (or LT) to see where it will land.

### Hot Potato (keyboard only, WIP)
- **Goal:** Toss the potato onto the other player's half before it sneezes confetti. Whoever has it (or has it on their side) when it goes off gives the other player a point. First to 2 rounds.
- **Controls:** WASD to move, walk up to the potato to pick it up, throw to toss it across.

### Breakout (keyboard only)
- **Goal:** Run as far as you can down a three-lane orchard course. You run on your own and speed up as you go. Jump the low logs and switch lanes around the tall hay bales.
- **Controls:** A / D (or left / right) to change lanes, Space to jump.

### Boulder Dash (keyboard only)
- **Goal:** Race up a steep channel while boulders roll down. Big grey boulders fill the channel, so duck into a lit recess; orange ones roll down one side, so step to the other. First to the top wins.
- **Controls:** WASD to move, Shift to sprint.

### Sprinkler Dodge
- **Goal:** Sprinklers pop up inside yellow warning circles. Get out of the circle before they spray. More sprinklers each wave. Last one dry wins.
- **Controls:** WASD or left stick to move. On a touch screen, drag anywhere to move; the round button bottom left pauses.

### Beach Ball Dodge (WIP)
- **Goal:** Stay on the pool float while bots throw beach balls across it. A pink line shows a ball's path just before the throw, so step out of it. First one knocked into the pool loses.
- **Controls:** WASD or left stick to move. On a touch screen, drag anywhere to move; the round button bottom left pauses.

## Standalone games

### Acorn Drop
- **Goal:** Drop acorns down the garden fence. They bounce off the pegs into flower pots, and each pot grows one to five flowers. The middle pot grows five!
- **Controls:** A / D or ← / → to walk along the fence (or tap where to go), Space or Enter to drop. Controller: stick walks, A drops.

### Animal Patrol
- **Goal:** Catch every critter in six scenes. If one hides behind a rock or tree, wait for it to come out.
- **Controls:** Tap a critter to throw a capture ball, or swipe from the ball to the critter. Keyboard: aim with WASD or arrows, throw with Space; 1–4 or R pick a ball. Controller: stick aims, A / RT throws, X / LT changes ball.

### Backyard Apple Catch
- **Goal:** Move your basket to catch apples and stay out from under the pinecones (they cost apples). Most apples at the end wins. Watch for the branch that shakes just before something falls.
- **Controls:** Hold A / D or ← / → (or press and hold either side of the screen) to move the basket left or right; let go to return to the middle.

### Backyard Fling (WIP)
- **Goal:** Your car zooms down the stunt track and round the loop by itself, then flies off the ramp into towers of wooden blocks. Knock every tower down to clear the yard.
- **Controls:** On the ramp, A / D or ← / → steer (or hold a side of the screen), W / S or ↑ / ↓ go faster or slower. The glowing ring shows where you'll land.

### Backyard Flier
- **Goal:** Keep your toy helicopter in the air and fly through the gaps between branches and shrubs. Each gap scores one; touching anything ends the flight.
- **Controls:** Tap the screen, or press Space, ↑ or W, to give it a little lift.

### Big Bad Burger
- **Goal:** Build the tallest burger you can. Drop each ingredient right on top for a Perfect drop, and balance the stack so it doesn't topple. It speeds up as it grows.
- **Controls:** Space, tap or click to drop. Controller: A.

### Bocce Ball
- **Goal:** Roll your balls as close as you can to the white pallino, or knock your rival's away. After four balls each, you score a point for each ball closer than their best. First to seven wins.
- **Controls:** Aim with ← → or drag the view, then hold Space (A on a controller) and release to roll. The grid dots show which way the ground slopes.

### Comet Dash
- **Goal:** Your runner races across floating sky islands. Jump the gaps and dash through glowing crystals. Hitting a crystal or an island's side, or falling, costs a star.
- **Controls:** Space, W or ↑ to jump (hold for higher, press again for a double jump). D, R, →, Shift or X to dash. Touch: left half jumps, right half dashes. Controller: A jumps, X dashes.

### Fire & Ice
- **Goal:** Pick fire or ice. Each round, choose Attack, Reload or Shield while the computer picks at the same time. An attack hits someone who is reloading; a shield blocks it. After attacking you must reload. First to five stars wins.
- **Controls:** Tap a button, or press 1–3. Controller: stick to choose, A to pick.

### Firefly Recall
- **Goal:** Watch the fireflies flash around the campfire, then repeat the pattern in order. Each round adds one flash. Keep going until you make a mistake. No time limit.
- **Controls:** Tap or click each firefly, or use the arrow keys. Controller: Y X B A match the colours.

### Kickabout
- **Goal:** 3 v 3 backyard soccer. Most goals in 3 minutes wins; a tie goes to golden goal.
- **Controls:** WASD or arrows to run. With the ball: hold Space to power up a shot and let go to shoot (hold ↑ / ↓ to aim high or low), R or E to pass. Without it: run into them, Space to lunge, R to switch player. Controller: stick runs, A shoots or lunges, X passes or switches. Touch: drag on the left side, buttons on the right.

### Kite Day (WIP)
- **Goal:** Fly your kite through the floating ribbons on a windy afternoon. Hold to reel in and climb, let go to let string out and drift farther. Watch the windsock for gusts and stay clear of the oak, the power line, the hedges and the crow. Gold ribbons are worth three. Two players: crossed strings tangle!
- **Controls:** A / D or ← / → steer, hold Space (or W / ↑) to reel in, R for a quick tug. Touch: ◀ ▶ steer, hold Reel (double-tap to tug). Controller: stick steers, hold A to reel, X tugs. Two players: player one A / D and Space, player two ← / → and ↑, or one controller each.

### Kubb · Viking Chess
- **Goal:** Knock down your rival's kubbs (wooden blocks), then topple the king to win. Hit the king too early and you lose.
- **Controls:** Aim with WASD, arrows, either stick, or drag the field. Hold Space (A / RT, or the Throw button), then release to throw.

### Lantern Trail
- **Goal:** Run, jump and stomp through three short woodland levels.
- **Controls:** A / D or arrows to run, Space, W or ↑ to jump (hold for more height). Controller: stick or D-pad and A.

### Leaf Piles
- **Goal:** Four players share a yard, one corner each. Rake falling leaves into your corner (and out of everyone else's). Whatever's in your corner when time runs out is your pile. Golden leaves are worth extra. First to win enough rounds wins.
- **Controls:** WASD or arrows to walk, Space to rake the leaves in front of you. Grab the leaf blower when it appears and hold Space to blow.

### Look & Find
- **Goal:** Explore busy pictures and find the hidden animals, toys and surprises. No timer, no penalty.
- **Controls:** Tap things in the picture. Tap a clue to hear it. Choose One at a time or Picture checklist. Keyboard / controller: arrows or WASD move the blue ring, Space or A picks, R repeats the clue, H gives a hint.

### Loop & Glide (keyboard only, WIP)
- **Goal:** The car launches itself through the loop. Glide it onto a picnic target to score. Targets move after each launch; three misses end the game.
- **Controls:** A / D steer, W dives to go farther, S flares to land closer, Space drops straight down.

### Paper Plane Pitch (WIP)
- **Goal:** Throw paper planes off the porch into the laundry basket. Watch the windsock: the breeze pushes the plane sideways, so aim into it. The basket moves after every throw.
- **Controls:** Swipe up from the plane (direction aims, a longer swipe throws harder). Or A / D to aim, W / S for power, Space to throw. Controller: stick aims and sets power, A throws.

### Perch Hop
- **Goal:** Land on all ten perches around the backyard as fast as you can. Each flag goes up in your colour. Avoid the pond!
- **Controls:** A / D or ← / → to run, Space, W or ↑ to jump (again in the air for a double jump; hold for higher). S or ↓ drops through a ledge. Trampolines bounce you high. Touch: arrow buttons and the big jump button.

### Perfect Pizza
- **Goal:** Make the pizza in the customer's picture: dough, sauce, cheese, then count on the right toppings and bake it. Take it out when the ring is full, before it burns.
- **Controls:** WASD or arrows to walk, E or Space to use what you're next to. Touch: the arrows (or tap where to walk) and the ✋ button. Controller: stick walks, A uses.

### Pipe Crane
- **Goal:** Use the crane to lay pipe in the trenches from pipe A to every other pipe before the water reaches the end. Make sure every open end joins up! Little Builders mode fits the pipes for you.
- **Controls:** Tap a stack to grab a pipe, ⟳ (or R) to turn it, tap a trench to lower it. Tap a laid pipe to turn it, or drag it somewhere else. Keys: arrows or WASD move the hook, Space grabs and drops, R / Q turn, G turns the water on early.

### Pond Fishing
- **Goal:** Cast into the pond and reel in when the bobber dunks under and the ! appears. Little nibbles are fake-outs. Longer casts find bigger fish. Ten casts.
- **Controls:** Aim with the mouse, a tap, or the arrow keys; cast and reel with a click, tap, Space or Enter.

### Pool Noodle Chop
- **Goal:** Swing your foam sword as the stripe on the falling noodle passes. Cutting right through the stripe scores the most; cutting above it or missing loses points. One swing per noodle. Highest total wins.
- **Controls:** Space or tap to swing.

### Pop Patrol
- **Goal:** Bubbles float along the path to your back door. Place backyard defenders to pop them all before they get there.
- **Controls:** Pick a defender (or press 1–5) and click the lawn to place it. Click a defender to upgrade, change its target, or sell it. ▶ (N) starts a wave, ⏩ (F) speeds up. Keyboard: arrows / WASD move the cursor, Space places. Controller: stick moves, A picks, X starts the wave, B backs out.

### Puck Clash
- **Goal:** Place a bumper, then both players shoot pucks at the same time. Each puck must reach the other half or hit a rival puck; knock rival pucks off the open sides. Most points after all rounds wins.
- **Controls:** Tap or drag the launch line (or J / L) to move, ← → to aim, ↑ ↓ for power, Q / E for curl, Space to lock your shot. Controller: right stick moves, left stick aims and sets power, X curls, A / RT locks.

### Racetrack Builder
- **Goal:** Build a toy-car track with loops and boosters that comes back round to the start, then push the car and see if it makes it all the way.
- **Controls:** Click a square to start (Rotate turns it), then add pieces from the open end. Press Test track and choose how hard to push. Too slow stalls on climbs; too fast flies off curves.

### Ready or Not
- **Goal:** Hide-and-seek in a house with a front yard and backyard. Hiding: stay untagged until time runs out. Seeking: find and tag all three critters.
- **Controls:** Move around, and press Space at a hiding spot (closet, bush, under a bed or couch) to hide.

### River Crossing
- **Goal:** Hop across the river on drifting branches and rocks without falling in. Three crossings.
- **Controls:** Arrow keys or WASD, tap where you want to hop, or the arrow buttons. The ring shows where a forward hop lands: green means safe.

### Roof Catch
- **Goal:** Catch fruit rolling off the tin roof. Dodge the pebbles and mud. Miss three fruit and it's over.
- **Controls:** A / D, arrow keys, or drag to steer.

### Sea Sweeper
- **Goal:** Clean up the beach before the tide comes back. Carry trash to the green bin and seashells to the pink bucket, one at a time. Your score is the trash; shells (and the rare golden shell) are a bonus.
- **Controls:** Walk with WASD, arrows, the stick, or drag on the screen. Walk up to something to pick it up.

### Shadow Shop
- **Goal:** Turn two odd objects until their combined shadow matches the silhouette.
- **Controls:** Drag an object left or right to turn it. The nearby buttons, or A / D and J / L, make smaller turns.

### Shoo Away
- **Goal:** Squirrels and sly foxes sneak up on your picnic, but they freeze while you're looking at them. Shoo them away once they're inside the white ring. Lose three snacks and the picnic's over.
- **Controls:** Drag to look around (or A / D to turn, W / S to look up and down). Tap a critter to shoo it, or Space / Enter shoos the closest one. Tap the corner map to turn that way. Controller: stick looks, A shoos.

### Shuffleboard
- **Goal:** Slide pucks down the table into the scoring zones. Only pucks farther than the other side's best score. First to 15 wins. Play the CPU or a friend.
- **Controls:** A / D or drag to aim. Space or tap starts the power meter; press again to throw.

### Sink or Float
- **Goal:** Hop across the pond on stepping stones. Some float, some sink, and they all look the same, so remember which ones sank. Cross three ponds with as few splashes as you can.
- **Controls:** WASD or arrows to hop (Space hops forward), tap a nearby stone, or the arrow buttons. Controller: stick or D-pad, A hops forward.

### Skee Ball
- **Goal:** Roll the ball up the lane into the rings: 10 to 50 points, or 100 in the little corner pockets. Nine balls a game.
- **Controls:** A / D or ← / → to aim, hold Space and let go for power. Or swipe up the lane (faster swipe rolls harder). Controller: stick aims, A rolls.

### Slipstream (keyboard only)
- **Goal:** Auto-sprint race against three rivals. Dive onto the blue slip 'n slides for speed and avoid the mud.
- **Controls:** A / D to steer, Space to dive (diving on grass is a belly flop that stops you). I J K L to look. Player two: arrows to steer, ↑ to dive.

### Sumo Wrestling
- **Goal:** Four-critter ring-out. Push everyone else over the edge. Last one inside wins.
- **Controls:** WASD or stick to move. Keyboard: Space dash, E grab / counter, hold Q to brace, R dodge. Controller: A dash, B grab / counter, hold X to brace, Y dodge. Press grab again when the counter prompt appears.

### Target Blaster
- **Goal:** Soak cans, bottles and sliding bullseyes with a water blaster. Bullseyes near the middle score more. Three timed waves.
- **Controls:** Aim with the mouse, a tap, or arrows / WASD; fire with a click, tap, Space or Enter. Every shot uses pressure: pump with R, E, right-click or the Pump button. Controller: stick aims, A fires, X pumps.
